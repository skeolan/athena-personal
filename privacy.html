Athena Personal: Privacy policy

Athena Personal is used only by its owner, with the owner's own Google account.

Google data accessed

  • The calendar list, to choose which calendars to include.
  • Events on the selected calendars (read-only).
  • Tasks on the selected task lists. Tasks are changed only after the owner approves the exact change, and never deleted.

How it is used and stored

  • Data is used only to show the owner their own briefing and carry out changes they approved.
  • Sign-in credentials are stored encrypted on the owner's computer. A separate read-only credential may be stored as an encrypted secret in the owner's private GitHub repository, to send the owner scheduled reminders.
  • Calendar and task content is not stored, except in reminders the owner sends to themselves.
  • When the owner asks for it, briefing content is shown to the AI assistant the owner uses on their own computer.

Sharing

Google user data is not sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at Google third-party connections.
